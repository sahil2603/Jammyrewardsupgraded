How to play HiLo

  • A card is shown. Guess if the next one is higher or the same, or lower or the same. Ace is the lowest card, King the highest.
  • On an Ace you pick Higher or Same. On a King you pick Lower or Same.
  • Each button shows the chance and the multiplier you would reach. Riskier guesses pay more.
  • Keep going to grow the multiplier, or cash out after any correct guess. A wrong guess loses the bet.
  • Don't like the card? Skip it, before or during a round, for free.
  • Cards come from an endless deck, so every card has the same chance every time.
  • Return to player is 98%, however many guesses you make. Biggest win per round: 100,000 coins.

Keyboard

Space bets or cashes out. ↑ and ↓ guess, → skips.

Provably fair

Before you bet, the result is locked in: you see a hash of the server's secret seed. Your client seed and the round number are mixed in, and after the round the secret seed is shown so you can check the result yourself.
