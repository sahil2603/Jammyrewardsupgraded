How to play Mines

  • Pick how much to bet and how many mines hide on the 5×5 board (1 to 24).
  • Press Bet, then open tiles. Every gem raises your multiplier.
  • Cash out whenever you like. Hit a mine and the bet is lost.
  • More mines means fewer gems but much bigger multipliers.
  • Return to player is 98% for every setting. Biggest win per round: 100,000 coins.

Keyboard

Space bets or cashes out. R picks a random tile.

Provably fair

Before you bet, the result is locked in: you see a hash of the server's secret seed. Your client seed and the round number are mixed in, and after the round the secret seed is shown so you can check the result yourself.
