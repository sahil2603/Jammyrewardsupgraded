How to play Plinko

  • Choose your bet, the risk and how many rows of pegs (8 to 16).
  • Press Drop ball. The ball bounces left or right at every row and lands in a slot. You win your bet times that slot's multiplier.
  • Edge slots pay the most and are the hardest to hit. Higher risk means bigger edges and smaller middles.
  • Drop as many balls as you like, or use Auto drop. Risk and rows can be changed once all balls have landed.
  • Every setting returns about 98% (between 97.9% and 98.2%). The panel shows the exact figure. Biggest win per ball: 100,000 coins.
